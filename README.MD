# Phishing Defense: Security Awareness Training

Welcome to the **Phishing Defense Presentation & Awareness Training** package. This project provides a structured, modern 10-slide curriculum designed to equip organizations and employees with the awareness, analytical skills, and proactive instincts needed to identify and neutralize social engineering threats.

## 1. Purpose of the Training

Phishing remains the number one initial attack vector used by threat actors to compromise enterprise perimeters, deploy ransomware, and harvest sensitive corporate credentials.

The primary goal of this training is to bridge the gap between technical defenses (such as firewalls and spam filters) and the human firewall. By demystifying the psychological levers and visual tricks utilized by modern cybercriminals, this program transforms end-users from passive potential targets into active defenders who protect company data and infrastructure.

## 3. Quiz Format

The training incorporates a scenario-based **Interactive Knowledge Check** (Slide 09):

* **Structure**: 5 focused, real-life situational prompts paired with immediate, color-coded defensive actions.

* **Scenarios Evaluated**:

  1. *Unsolicited urgent password resets* $\rightarrow$ Direct navigation rather than clicking links.

  2. *Subtly spoofed domains (`company-support.cloud`)* $\rightarrow$ Identification of lookalike infrastructure.

  3. *Delivery notifications via SMS (Smishing)* $\rightarrow$ Immediate deletion and validation bypass.

  4. *Physical QR code overlays on public terminals (Quishing)* $\rightarrow$ Physical inspection and meter payment.

  5. *Accidental credential entry* $\rightarrow$ Emergency incident response (immediate password resets and SecOps escalation).

* **Objective**: Shifts assessment from abstract theory to muscle memory, enabling learners to make split-second defensive decisions.

## 4. How the Material Helps Employees Recognize Phishing

1. **Practical Deconstruction Over Theory**: Rather than simply advising people to "be careful," the course breaks down the mechanics of lookalike domains, fake subdomains (e.g., `microsoft.portal-verify.com`), and hidden payloads.

2. **Emotional Calibration**: Learners understand that when an email provokes feelings of anxiety, panic, or haste, those emotions are often deliberate manipulation triggers used by an attacker.

3. **Actionable Hesitation**: Employees learn practical validation steps—such as picking up the phone to perform an out-of-band check or navigating directly to an internal bookmark instead of trusting email links.

## 5. What You Have Learned

Completing and delivering this training yields four core competencies:

* **How Phishing Works**: Understanding the attacker's end-to-end playbook, from weaponizing communication channels to extracting credentials or session tokens.

* **How to Recognize Warning Signs**: Developing a sharp eye for typosquatting, spoofed headers, high-pressure deadlines, and unusual administrative demands.

* **How to Train Other Employees**: Gaining the visual tools, conversational frameworks, and case studies required to lead engaging security workshops and onboard colleagues.

* **How to Explain Cybersecurity Risks Clearly**: Translating complex infosec principles into plain, accessible, and actionable language that empowers non-technical teams without relying on technical jargon.
